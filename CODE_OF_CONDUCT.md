# Code of Conduct

Participants must communicate respectfully and protect the dignity, privacy,
and knowledge of Deaf people and SgSL communities.

Harassment, identity-based abuse, unwanted disclosure of personal information,
and extraction or publication of private community material are not accepted.
Disagreement about implementation or linguistic evidence should remain
specific, evidence-led, and open to review.

Project maintainers may edit, reject, hide, or remove contributions and
participation that violate these expectations.
